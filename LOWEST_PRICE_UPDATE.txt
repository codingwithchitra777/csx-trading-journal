🎯 WHAT CHANGED
═══════════════

The bot now matches sells with the LOWEST PRICED purchases first
(instead of LIFO - Last In First Out).

This MAXIMIZES your profit on each sale! 📈


💰 HOW IT WORKS
══════════════

When you sell shares, the system automatically finds your 
CHEAPEST purchases and sells those first.

Example:
  Buy #1: 100 @ 7,000 riel (cheapest)
  Buy #2: 100 @ 7,100 riel
  Buy #3: 100 @ 7,200 riel (most expensive)
  
  Sell: 150 @ 7,500 riel
  
  ✅ Matches with Buy #1 (100) and Buy #2 (50)
  ✅ Profit: 70,000 riel
  ✅ Buy #3 remains in your position


📊 PROFIT COMPARISON
═══════════════════

Same scenario, different matching methods:

  Lowest Price First: 70,000 riel ⭐ HIGHEST
  FIFO (oldest first): 70,000 riel
  LIFO (newest first): 50,000 riel ❌ LOWEST
