🎯 LIFO MATCHING
═══════════════

When you sell, it matches with YOUR MOST RECENT purchases first.

Example:
  Buy #1: 100 @ 7,000  (oldest)
  Buy #2: 100 @ 7,100
  Buy #3: 100 @ 7,200  (newest)
  
  Sell: 150 @ 7,500
  
  → Matches with Buy #3 (100) and Buy #2 (50)
  → Buy #1 remains untouched
  → Profit: 50,000 riel
